How registration works
Administrator controls for everyone in this workspace. Choose the allowed sign-in methods and registration rules. The first to complete the initial password admin, a password sign-up (verified or direct) or a Google sign-in became the administrator; after that, self-registration governs public sign-up and new accounts receive the restricted selfRegistered role. Password sign-up is available without email delivery by default; requiring email verification at sign-up restores the emailed-code step and needs working email delivery. Manage your own password, passkeys and authenticator app under Sign-in & security on My Account.
