RoboTeam Documentation

RoboTeam is the management space for a team of persistent virtual employees: specialized robots that keep their identity, working context, and tools between sessions.

Purpose and vision

RoboTeam gives an owner one place to assemble and supervise a roster of virtual employees. Each employee is a robot with a distinct role, a durable profile, a private workspace, and an interactive desktop. The owner can prepare the employee's browser, terminal, files, and authenticated sessions without losing that working context when the desktop stops.

The product vision treats these robots like members of an organization. Their names and specializations make responsibilities understandable to people, while their isolated workspaces keep each employee's context separate. RoboTeam manages the roster and the persistent working environments; AdvancedLanguageAgent supplies the separate one-shot runtime that can carry out a task with explicitly selected skills and profile resources.

The roles in the diagram are examples. An owner defines the size and specialization of the team, and every employee keeps a separate identity, workspace, browser state, and desktop. This model supports a growing organization without turning individual robots into separate public services or separate Ploinky agents.
