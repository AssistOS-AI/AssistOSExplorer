Authorization Boundaries

Tool presence is not authorization. webmeet_room_list and the workspace room feed are decided only on the router-signed direct user: a non-guest admin or a user holding the signed explorer.access capability. Other authenticated callers, including delegated calls and users whose only claim to administration is their username, receive an empty room list and no feed; archived rooms are listed only for a verified administrator, and canManageRooms reports that same verified role. Room operations validate the authenticated participant and room role; owner-bound guest join, room-state read, chat send, leave, heartbeat, and avatar operations require the router-issued exact room scope and the participant identity bound to that guest session; administrator operations enforce the verified administrator role; and internal secretary operations require the exact delegated agent principal.

The current public guest contract exposes minimal pre-join room metadata, admission, the narrowed post-join room projection, guest-authored chat, leave, heartbeat, and avatar publication. It does not expose authenticated room reads, general chat listing, resources, management, or room-agent controls. webmeet_room_delete additionally requires an authenticated administrator and explicit confirmation. SCRIPTA workspace discovery rejects guests, while collaboration operations resolve access against the participant and active room document.

Observable Results

Read operations return filtered room, participant, event, resource, Blackboard, or collaboration projections. Mutation operations return the accepted projection or an explicit structured error. Durable mutations emit the applicable central WebMeet event; realtime-only status signals are not written as persistent room history.
